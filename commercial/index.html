Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can result in carbon monoxide from a blocked or undersized vent, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Santa Cruz Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where local code calls for it, and the permit and inspection process. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.

Ongoing Protection

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

A maintenance program from Santa Cruz Water Heater is built around your equipment, how hard the system works, and your budget.
