Signs You Need Commercial Water Heaters in Santa Cruz

Most tanks announce trouble weeks or months ahead with rumbling noises, rusty water, or hot showers that keep getting shorter. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Rising gas or electric bills with no change in business volume
  • A heater old enough that a failure feels like a matter of time
  • Loud sediment noise from equipment that runs all day
  • A recirculation loop that no longer seems to do its job
  • Visible corrosion or water pooling near the tank
  • Dish or hand sink temperatures drifting below what your inspector expects
  • Fixtures that run too hot one minute and cool the next
  • Lukewarm water at the exact time you need the most

Water Heater Care Tips From Our Technicians

Routine maintenance is almost always cheaper and easier than an early replacement. Follow these tips from our technicians:

  • Replace consumed anode rods before the tank itself starts to corrode
  • Flush commercial tanks more often than residential ones, often every few months in heavy use or hard water
  • Log water temperatures at key fixtures so drift is caught early
  • Keep the area around the heaters clear for combustion air and service access
  • Confirm the recirculation loop still delivers hot water quickly to the far fixtures
  • Budget for replacement as equipment nears the end of its expected life
  • Verify delivery temperatures at sinks and showers a few times a year
